PharmacyOS is applied-platform work in its purest form: software for a business
that cannot tolerate downtime, confusion or ceremony. The system covers the
operational core of a pharmacy — stock, sales, records — and is built to run in
real conditions, on real hardware, for people whose job is not software.

It is the reference case for how AVATOR builds: complete systems, deployed and
iterated against reality.
