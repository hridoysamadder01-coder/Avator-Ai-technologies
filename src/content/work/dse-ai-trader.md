An experimental system exploring AI-assisted analysis of the Dhaka Stock
Exchange: ingesting market data, extracting signal, and honestly measuring
where models help and where they only appear to.

The project is run as research, not as a promise. Its real product is the data
and signal infrastructure built along the way — now feeding the wider AVATOR
platform.
