Every intelligent system is downstream of its data. AVATOR engineers the
unglamorous layer properly: ingestion from messy sources, cleaning, storage
shaped for retrieval, and the live signal surfaces that operators actually
watch.

Our signal work began in applied market analysis and operational dashboards,
and is growing into a shared data layer for the whole product line.
