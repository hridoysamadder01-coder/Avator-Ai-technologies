Language is the interface layer of the AVATOR ecosystem. We build systems where
language models do useful, bounded, verifiable work: answering from a company's
own knowledge, driving software through conversation, and turning unstructured
text into structured, auditable data.

Our language systems are engineered as products, not prompts — with evaluation,
fallbacks and observability designed in from the start. Implementation details
of our orchestration remain proprietary; what we publish is what the systems can
do, and the interfaces through which they do it.
