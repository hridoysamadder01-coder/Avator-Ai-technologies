Not everything AVATOR builds is a product yet. A standing experimental practice
runs ahead of the roadmap: evaluating what current models can genuinely be
trusted to do, prototyping systems at the edge of that boundary, and feeding
what survives back into the product line.

Findings are published as field notes when they are real, reproducible and safe
to share. Nothing is announced before it exists.
