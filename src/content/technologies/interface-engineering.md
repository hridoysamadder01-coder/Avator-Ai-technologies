Intelligence without interface is a lab result. AVATOR treats the surface of a
system — its speed, its typography, its behavior under a thumb on a cheap phone —
as core engineering, not decoration.

We build interfaces that make complex systems legible: what the machine did,
why, and what happens next. As AI systems take on more of the work, this
discipline becomes more important, not less.
