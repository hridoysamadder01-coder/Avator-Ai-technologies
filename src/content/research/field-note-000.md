This journal will carry AVATOR's research notes: evaluations, experiments,
engineering findings from building applied AI systems in conditions most labs
never test in.

The publishing rule is simple. A note appears here only when the work is real,
the result is reproducible, and sharing it doesn't hand over what makes our
systems ours. That means this page will grow slowly, and everything on it can
be trusted.
